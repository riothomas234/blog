AIxBio Research Fellow

July — September 2026

ERA

  • I wrote a workshop paper outlining the threat model and cryptographic design for a tamper-proof log in benchtop DNA synthesisers. Using a cryptographic merkle tree and trusted platform module, we can provide certain security guarantees against malicious operators who synthesize a viral DNA strand and want to modify the records to hide it.
  • This fellowship really empowered me as a researcher. I realised that for topics that interested me, e.g. safeguards in ML biology tools, I could just go and read the literature myself and after that, do something about it. Like, program a simulation or write a blog. The people here were great to be around. Now, I consider the work culture a much bigger factor when deciding where I want to work in the future - I think a big part of work are the bonds formed between people aiming for a common goal.

Summer Work Placement

July — September 2023

Moog Inc.

  • These guys made slip rings that connect circuits across rotary junctions, e.g. at a ball and socket junction in a robot. I did cool things like assembling a Bracchio robot arm and checking out their CubeSat in the clean room ready to be deployed to space. I also learnt how to use CAD - Creo - to design the mechanical case for a mobile phone. On the last day, I was whisked away to a big space conference which i forget the name of, but i got to see what space companies do and the extortionate prices charged for sandwiches at the lunch bar (£10!). Ultimately, this placement showed me engineering wasn't the right path for me. I cared more about understanding how mechanisms work rather than building something useful, so i pivoted to studying physics at undergrad. I also realised that being an engineer in a commercial context often involves more design and business thinking and less of the mathsy side which i missed.
